# Privacy Policy

DoNotMissIt does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

Calendar events are read on your Mac only to show meeting alerts and are never sent anywhere. The app has no analytics, advertising or accounts.

When you choose to join a meeting, its link opens in your browser or meeting app.

DoNotMissIt is open source. You can audit the code at https://github.com/stut/donotmissit.

Questions: https://github.com/stut/donotmissit/issues
